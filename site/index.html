Text and canvas

The file is the truth.

Edit the map or edit the file; they are the same thing. A drag writes one line. A rename touches one field. git diff shows exactly what changed about how the business runs.

1 - id: insurance-verification # uncertain: "two days out" was Karen's guess 2 type: process 3 label: Run daily verifications 4 cost: 5 runs: 220 6 human: {minutes: 14} 7 agent: {perRun: 0.42, setup: 2600} 8 # what it costs a person, what it would cost an agent, beside the step itself

The proof

Paste the discovery call. Review what it heard.

The importer builds the map only from what was said. Anything merely implied carries its reason for doubt until a human clears it. Try it:

Karendiscovery call, Brightside DentalMarisol runs the verifications every morning for anyone coming in the next… I think she works two days out?
- id: insurance-verification # uncertain: "two days out" was Karen's guess
⚑uncertain: "two days out" was Karen's guessCONFIRMED

A transcript with no process in it gets an error, not an invented map. Keys never reach the browser, the YAML, or an export, and the app runs fully offline with the importer switched off.

Your machine

One command.
No account.

Local-first: client data and transcripts never leave the machine. It needs Node 18 and two vendored libraries. Nothing to install, nothing to build.

The file is the truth.
